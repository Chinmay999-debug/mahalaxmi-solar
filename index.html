(04)How we work

Seven steps, from your first call to after-sales service.

  1. We discuss your property, your electricity bill and what you want from solar.

  2. Our team visits to check the roof or land — structure, direction and shade.

  3. We recommend the system size, layout, on-grid or hybrid setup and components.

  4. We procure the equipment and install the system on site.

  5. PM Surya Ghar subsidy paperwork and electricity-board applications.

  6. The system is tested, switched on and handed over to you.

  7. One year of free AMC, and service whenever you need it after that.
